Introduction

The CSE AIML Department is dedicated to providing high-quality education and research opportunities in the field of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. Our faculty members are experts in their respective areas, and we offer a range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs to prepare students for successful careers in AI and ML.

Academic Rules

  • Students must attend classes regularly.
  • Students should come to class on time.
  • Students should carry their college ID.
  • Students should complete their assignments.

Important: Students must check the timetable regularly for any changes.

Before Practical Class

  1. Read the practical topic.
  2. Bring the required notebook.
  3. Attend the practical class.
  4. Complete the practical work.

Classroom Rules

  • Do not make noise.
  • Keep the classroom clean.
  • Listen to the teacher.
  • Do not use phones during class.
