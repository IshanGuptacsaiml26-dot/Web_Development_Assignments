Agra Travel Guide

Agra is a famous city in India. It is famous for the Taj Mahal.

1. Places to Visit

The Taj Mahal is the most famous place in Agra.

Agra Fort is another famous place to visit.

2. Culture

Agra has a rich history and beautiful old buildings.

3. Food

Agra is famous for a sweet called Petha.

4. Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Agra is from October to March.

5. Travel Tips

Important Tip: Wear comfortable shoes and carry water.
