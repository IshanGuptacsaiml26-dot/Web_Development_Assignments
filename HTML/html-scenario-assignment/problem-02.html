Heavy Rain in Delhi-NCR

Heavy rain causes waterlogging and traffic problems

Heavy rain caused waterlogging in Delhi-NCR. Many people had problems while travelling on the roads.

The rain affected many areas of Delhi and nearby places. Some roads had a lot of water and traffic became slow.

The heavy rain happened on 20 August 2026. People faced delays while going to school, college and work.

The main reason for the problem was heavy monsoon rain. People were advised to be careful while travelling.
